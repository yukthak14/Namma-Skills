# 📱 About the App

**Namma-Skill** is an AI-powered Android application developed to bridge the gap between rural youth and government skill development centers.  

Many students and unemployed youth in villages and small towns miss valuable vocational training opportunities because they are unaware of upcoming short-term skill batches.

This application acts as a **Training Opportunity Tracker**, helping users discover nearby government skill centers, explore vocational courses, apply instantly, and receive notifications about new training programs.

The platform focuses on improving employment opportunities and making skill development accessible for everyone.

---

# 🎯 Problem Statement

There are many government and private skill development centers offering short-term vocational training programs such as:

- Electrician Training
- Mobile Repair
- Tailoring & Sewing
- Computer Courses
- Coding & IT Skills

However, youth in rural areas often do not know:

- When a batch starts
- Eligibility criteria
- Available seats
- Nearby training centers
- Job guarantee information

Due to lack of awareness, many opportunities are missed, leading to unemployment and lack of career growth.

**Namma-Skill** solves this issue by providing a centralized platform for discovering and applying to skill development programs.
